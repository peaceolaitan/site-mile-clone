Frequently Asked Questions

WordPress agency FAQs

Answers to the questions businesses ask before hiring our WordPress agency - redesigns, WooCommerce, speed, support, SEO foundations and custom development.

Can you redesign my existing WordPress website without losing my content? ^

Yes. Our WordPress agency can redesign your existing website while keeping important pages, blog posts, media and SEO-friendly structure intact. We review your current site first, protect key content and rankings, then create a cleaner, more modern WordPress website design that improves performance and user experience.

Do you build custom WordPress websites or mostly work from templates? ^

We focus on custom WordPress website development for businesses that want a site tailored to their brand, goals and workflow. That means better design flexibility, cleaner code, stronger performance and more room for advanced functionality than a typical off-the-shelf template.

Can your team handle WooCommerce development for online stores? ^

Absolutely. We provide WooCommerce development services for businesses that need custom product pages, checkout improvements, payment gateway integration, shipping rules, store design and ongoing support. Whether launching a new store or upgrading an old one, we build WooCommerce websites that are easier to manage and better for conversions.

Do you offer custom plugin development and advanced WordPress functionality? ^

Yes. Our WordPress development team builds custom plugins, API integrations, member areas, booking systems, custom dashboards and other advanced features when a standard plugin isn't enough. This is ideal for businesses that need real custom WordPress development instead of a brochure site.

Will my new WordPress website be SEO friendly from the start? ^

Yes. We build SEO-friendly WordPress websites with strong page structure, clean code, mobile responsiveness, proper headings, fast loading times and a solid technical foundation. This gives your business a better base for WordPress SEO, content growth, local SEO and future ranking improvements.

How long does a typical WordPress website project take? ^

It depends on the size of the project, the number of pages, design requirements and whether custom development is involved. A smaller WordPress website design project may move faster than a large WooCommerce or custom plugin build. We define the scope early so you know what's included and the timeline.

Do you provide WordPress maintenance and support after launch? ^

Yes. We offer WordPress maintenance and support after launch to keep your website updated, secure and running smoothly — plugin updates, bug fixes, performance checks, backups, content edits, malware cleanup and technical help.

Can you improve website speed and Core Web Vitals on WordPress? ^

Yes. WordPress speed optimization is an important part of our work. We improve code quality, images, caching, scripts, layout stability and general front-end performance to help your site load faster. Better speed supports conversions, UX and the technical side of SEO.

Can you migrate my website to WordPress from another platform? ^

Yes. We migrate websites from other platforms into WordPress while preserving important content, URLs where possible and key SEO elements. This is useful for businesses moving from website builders or outdated systems into a more flexible WordPress setup that's easier to grow.

Why hire a WordPress agency instead of a freelance WordPress developer? ^

Hiring a WordPress agency gives you access to a wider skill set: strategy, design, development, WooCommerce expertise, SEO, testing and long-term support. Especially useful for businesses that need a reliable team for custom WordPress development rather than a one-person freelance setup.
